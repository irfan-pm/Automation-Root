About MMG

Manufacturing Quality Polythene Since 2005

Since 2005, MMG Factories Management for Plastics has grown into a leading Qatar-based manufacturer of eco-friendly polythene sheets, warning tapes and all kinds of plastic bags, including a wide range of oxo-biodegradable bags for retail, food, hospitality, healthcare, construction and agriculture.

Cutting-edge machinery gives us a monthly production capacity of 10,000 metric tons. We produce to your precise specifications (size, colour, thickness and printing), work around the clock, and supply with punctuality and strict quality control across Qatar, the GCC and export markets.

  • Eco-friendly, oxo-biodegradable product range
  • Custom sizes, colours, thickness & printed designs
  • Certified to regional safety & quality requirements
  • Exporting to the Middle East, Europe & Asia
